YOUR MODEL. YOUR HARDWARE. YOUR FACTORY.

Big jobs. Chicken problems.

01 / WORKER

One job at a time.

The factory gives the model a focused station: inspect the project, make a change, or build a check. Clear inputs. Concrete work.

02 / SUPERVISOR

Make the claim earn its pass.

The model can review the work and design a test. The factory executes the check, binds it to the current files, and directs the next repair or completion.

03 / IMPROVEMENT

Equip the next job.

Build reusable tools for recurring work. The experimental self-improvement cycle can build and test changes to the factory itself.

How to read a fight card Clocks, checks & conditions +

PASS requires both accepted work and clean completion. OUTPUT_ONLY means the artifact passed but the run did not finish cleanly. A timeout is not time to successful completion. Missing token counters remain unknown, never zero.

The roster shows which systems ran each task. A solo card contains one recorded attempt. Completed comparisons include every planned contender. These are selected development work orders; the recording dates and conditions are attached to each card.

Time bars show elapsed run time, not progress or correctness. Local and frontier models are distinguished in the replay. Read each card's conditions for configuration and accounting details. Open a contender to read its actions, inspect its delivered files and see its test results. Cards with a demo also let you try the recorded tool. This static site loads no external assets and sends no telemetry.
