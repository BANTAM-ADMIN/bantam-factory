# Palimpsest launch memo

Palimpsest is a private desktop writing companion that keeps a local,
searchable history of a manuscript's revisions. It can compare passages,
surface discarded phrases, and create a compact change map. Its optional cloud
sync is not part of this launch.

Audience: novelists and long-form nonfiction writers who care about language,
privacy, and understanding how a draft evolved.

Editorial wants wonder and emotional texture. Legal wants precise claims.
Product wants the revision map mentioned. Support insists we say that semantic
search is limited while offline and improves only after local indexing has
finished. Nobody wants generic software hype.

The copy must not imply that Palimpsest writes the book, understands authorial
intent, or guarantees recovery from disk failure.
