# Orbitseed launch visual

Create a premium editorial campaign image for a fictional pocket planetarium
called **Orbitseed**.

Required visual story:

- one compact brass-and-midnight-blue device, small enough for a hand;
- a projected miniature solar system hovering above it;
- exactly one child and one older adult sharing the discovery;
- a cozy apartment at night, not a laboratory;
- wonder without neon sci-fi clichés;
- usable negative space for copy;
- no words, letters, logos, watermark, or interface text in the image.

The image should feel tactile, intimate, and believable enough for a design
magazine advertisement.
