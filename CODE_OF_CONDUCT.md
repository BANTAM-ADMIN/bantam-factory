# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be decent. Argue with the work, not the person.

This project runs on measurements, which makes most disagreements settleable:
bring the A/B, the failure film, or the seam test, and the evidence carries the
argument. "I think this is bad" is a weak claim here regardless of who says it,
and so is "I think this is good."

Unacceptable, in issues, pull requests, reviews, or anywhere else the project
gathers:

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments about someone rather than
  their patch.
- Harassment, sustained or otherwise, public or private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately wasting reviewers' time — spam PRs, generated slop submitted
  without reading it, repeatedly reopening settled decisions without new
  evidence.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through GitHub:
[**Security → Report a vulnerability**](https://github.com/BANTAM-ADMIN/bantam-factory/security/advisories/new)
also reaches the maintainers privately and is the right channel until this
project has a dedicated one. Say what happened and link what you can.

Reports are handled privately. Reporting in good faith will never be held
against you, whatever the outcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that violate this
document, and may block accounts. Responses scale with severity: a warning
first where a warning is plausibly enough, an immediate ban where it is not.
