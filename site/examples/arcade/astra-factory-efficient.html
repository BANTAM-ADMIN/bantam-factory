How to play & keyboard controls

Complete horizontal rows to clear them. Clear 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 rows for 100 / 300 / 500 / 800 × level points; consecutive clears earn a combo bonus. Every 10 lines raises the level and the pace. The outline shows where your piece will land. Hold once per piece. Stack to the top and the round ends.

← → move · ↑ / X rotate clockwise · Z rotate counterclockwise · ↓ soft drop · Space hard drop · C / Shift hold · P / Esc pause. On a phone, use the buttons; hold left, right, or down to repeat. Switching away automatically pauses. Best score stays on this device when storage is available.
